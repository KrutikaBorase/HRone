# REVIEW.md

| # | Where (function / line) | What is wrong | How you'd notice it (test, input, or symptom) | How you fixed it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `compute_late_minutes` | It compares elapsed whole minutes to 10, so 09:40:01 is incorrectly inside the grace period; it also does not explicitly anchor shift start to IST or the attendance day. | For a 09:30 shift, punch at 09:40:01 and expect 10 late minutes; repeat for an overnight shift. | Truncate instants to seconds, derive attendance day in IST, anchor shift start to that day, and test the 600-second grace threshold before flooring minutes. |
| 2 | `compute_work_hours` | Built-in `round` uses ties-to-even rather than the required half-up rounding. | Exercise a duration whose hours are exactly halfway at the second decimal place. | Use `Decimal` with `ROUND_HALF_UP` after calculating whole elapsed seconds. |
| 3 | `compute_overtime` | It compares against a same-date shift end even for overnight shifts and counts any positive minute rather than requiring at least 30 minutes. | Punch out before an overnight shift's next-day end, or 29 minutes after a regular shift end. | Calculate the shift end on the correct calendar day and return overtime only at 30 minutes or more. |
| 4 | Mongo client and startup | The client returns naive datetimes and creates no required indexes; duplicate attendance and employee codes are therefore not protected under concurrency. | Inspect startup indexes, and send identical concurrent punch-ins or employee creates. | Use a timezone-aware client and idempotent unique/analytics indexes at startup. |
| 5 | `health` | It returns success without checking MongoDB. | Stop MongoDB and call `/health`; the starter still says `ok`. | Ping MongoDB and return 503 when the database is unavailable. |
| 6 | `create_employee` | The preflight duplicate check is racy, creation time is naive, and the response returns a datetime instead of epoch milliseconds. Request values also lack the contract's field/date/shift validation. | Submit invalid fields or duplicate codes concurrently; inspect `created_at` in the response. | Validate the request, rely on the unique index for atomic uniqueness, store UTC time, and serialize it as epoch milliseconds. |
| 7 | `list_employees` | Pagination skips one extra page (`page * page_size`), `total` ignores the department filter, and results are not sorted. | Request page 1 and page 2 with a known department subset. | Use `(page - 1) * page_size`, count the filtered query, and sort by `emp_code`. |
| 8 | `punch_in` | Unknown employees cause a server error; `punched_at` is interpreted as seconds and local-naive time; date/status/race rules are not enforced. | Use an unknown code, epoch milliseconds, an overnight employee, an invalid status, or simultaneous requests. | Validate the employee and body, convert/truncate UTC epoch milliseconds, derive the IST attendance date, accept presence statuses only, and let the unique natural-key index arbitrate races. |
| 9 | `list_attendance` | It loads and sorts every matching record in Python, which does not scale to the required dataset; it does not provide the required secondary sort key. | Seed many records and inspect query work/memory, or create multiple employees' records on one date. | Count using the same filtered query and apply indexed sort, skip, and limit in MongoDB. |
| 10 | API serialization | Attendance/employee instants and nested history dates are not consistently converted to epoch milliseconds; seeded documents may omit fields the response requires. | Read an employee, an attendance record, or a correction history entry. | Centralize recursive datetime conversion, exclude `_id`, and apply documented defaults for missing derived fields/history. |
| 11 | Missing punch-out endpoint | No endpoint closes the latest eligible record or atomically prevents two successful punch-outs. | Punch out across midnight, repeat a punch-out, or race two requests. | Implement a latest-eligible-record lookup, range checks, derived calculations, and conditional atomic update. |
| 12 | Missing regularization endpoint | No corrections, recomputation, audit trail, or optimistic concurrency protection exist. | Correct a timestamp/status, submit a no-op, or race two edits. | Validate final state, recompute derived values, record only actual field changes, append one history entry, and condition the update on the previous history. |
| 13 | Missing analytics endpoints | Monthly, department, leaderboard, and trend analytics required by R5-R9 are absent. | Call each documented analytics route, especially with half-days, weekend records, tied ranks, zero-log employees, and date gaps. | Add MongoDB aggregations for each contract, include eligible employees in department headcount, and gap-fill daily trends in MongoDB. |
| 14 | Missing explain endpoint and query support | No endpoint exposes query plans, and the starter's unindexed in-memory attendance query cannot meet the required plan/performance contract. | Call each explain variant on a large seeded dataset. | Share aggregation/query builders with the endpoints and explain those operations using execution statistics. |

The `compute_work_hours` duration formula itself (elapsed punch-out minus punch-in) is correct; the defect is its rounding mode. The starter's inclusive date predicates were also correct, but applying pagination after loading the entire result set was not. No API-provided employee `_id` was needed: the contract deliberately uses `emp_code` as the business key.
